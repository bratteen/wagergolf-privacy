## What is Split Sixes?

Split Sixes is the three-player format above all others. Six points are available on every hole, awarded according to how you finish that hole. It means something is at stake on every single hole, and a strong third place still bleeds points. The tension holds all the way round.

## How to play, and how the points divide

Six points per hole, awarded by position:

- Outright first: 4 points. Outright second: 2 points. Outright third: 0 points.
- Tied first: the leaders share 4 plus 2, so 3 points each. Third gets 0.
- Tied second: first takes 4, the two in second share 2, so 1 point each.
- All three tied: 6 points shared, 2 points each.

Split Sixes can be played off gross or net depending on how evenly matched the group is.

## How it is settled

At the end of the round everyone's points are totalled. The settlement is pairwise: each pair of players is compared, and the points difference times the stake changes hands. Whoever collected the most takes from the other two, and it all balances out. With three players the swings are substantial, which is part of the charm.
