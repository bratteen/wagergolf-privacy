## What is best ball?

Best ball, or four-ball, is the team format where you keep the most freedom. You play your own ball from tee to hole, exactly as in an ordinary round, but you have a partner to catch you when it goes wrong. Only the team's better score on each hole counts, so you can take turns being the hero.

## How to play best ball

- All four players play their own ball for the whole round.
- On each hole, only the better score in each team counts.
- The team with the lower best ball wins the hole.
- An equal best ball halves the hole.

## How it is worked out

Best ball is most often settled as a match between the two teams. Win the hole and your team goes one up, and once the lead is greater than the number of holes remaining the match is over, exactly as in ordinary match play.

Off handicap, each player has their strokes allocated according to the course's stroke index, and the net scores determine the team's best ball on the hole. That makes best ball an excellent choice for four players of differing standards. Wager Golf works out each player's net score, picks the team's best ball and keeps the match score for you.

## When does best ball suit?

Best ball is the perfect choice when four players of differing standards want to play together and still compete on equal terms. Everyone plays their own ball, so nobody has to feel like a liability, and a hot day from the team's weaker player can decide the whole match.

## Tactics and common mistakes

- **Both players should dare.** Because only the best ball counts, it is a waste if both play safe. If your partner is already well placed, go for the flag.
- **Take turns taking the risks.** If one of you knows your partner has a par in the bag, the other can attack without fear. That is where best ball is won, not in both of you playing carefully.
