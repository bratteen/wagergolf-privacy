## What is Wolf?

Wolf is one of the most enjoyable games for three or four players. A wolf is appointed on every hole, and the wolf decides how the hole will be played. Team up with a partner, or take on everyone else alone? That tactical choice, hole after hole, is what makes Wolf so addictive.

## How to play Wolf

- The wolf role rotates, a new wolf every hole.
- Players tee off in turn. The wolf goes last.
- After each tee shot the wolf can choose that player as a partner, but must decide immediately, before the next player hits.
- If the wolf chooses nobody, they play Lone Wolf, alone against everyone else.
- If the wolf wants a real gamble, they can call Blind Wolf before even hitting their own tee shot.

## How the units are counted

Each hole is settled on the teams' best ball. If the wolf's side wins, everyone on that side gets a unit. If the wolf plays alone and wins it is worth double, two units. A Blind Wolf that comes off is worth three. If the wolf loses, the units go to the opposing side instead.

On the closing holes the rotation changes character: whoever is furthest behind becomes the wolf, so a player who has fallen back gets a chance to make it up with a bold Lone or Blind Wolf.
